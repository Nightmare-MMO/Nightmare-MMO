# Nightmare MMO

Welcome to the official launch of **Nightmare MMO** — a retro-inspired MMORPG project focused on bringing back classic online RPG gameplay with modern improvements, active development, and a dedicated community.

Opening our doors June 1st - open sourcing at the same time!

Built for players who miss the golden era of MMORPGs, Nightmare MMO aims to deliver:

* Open-world exploration
* Classic RPG progression
* PvP & PvE content
* Bosses, quests, and rare loot
* Community-driven updates
* Retro MMO atmosphere

The original *Nightmare MMO* featured a large seamless world, multiple playable classes, crafting systems, PvP mechanics, and extensive character progression. ([itch.io][1])

## Vision

Nightmare MMO is a community-focused relaunch designed to preserve the feel of old-school MMORPGs while improving stability, balance, and long-term support.

The goal is simple:

> Build an MMO that feels like the classics — social, challenging, rewarding, and alive.
